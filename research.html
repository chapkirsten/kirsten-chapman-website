01
RESEARCH

My work asks how we can build technology that actually accounts for the people using it.

What

My research focuses on privacy, social computing, responsible AI, accessibility, and people's experiences with emerging technologies.

Why

I'm interested in where technology fails to account for the people using it, particularly when those experiences are overlooked in design and evaluation.

How

I use qualitative, quantitative, and mixed-methods approaches, including interviews, surveys, and diary studies.
