01
ABOUT

My work asks how we can build technology that actually accounts for the people using it.

Currently

I'm a PhD candidate at Brigham Young University, where I work with Dr. Xinru Page in the Social Technology and Privacy Lab (STaPL).

What I study

My research focuses on privacy, social computing, responsible AI, and people's experiences with emerging technologies.

How I study it

I use qualitative, quantitative, and mixed-methods approaches, including interviews, surveys, and diary studies.
